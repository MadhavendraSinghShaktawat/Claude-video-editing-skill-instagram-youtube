## Concept in one paragraph
A product explainer for a business-finance tool, in black on warm off-white, with almost no cuts. A single black square is the recurring actor: it opens the film by shrinking out of a black frame, becomes a point on a timeline, a bar, a word, the dot of an "i", a bar chart, a shrunken app window, four bank accounts, and the logo at the end. Between those, white UI panels of the product slide in, are shown briefly with a caption beside them, then collapse back into the square. The whole piece reads as one continuous canvas that morphs.

## Patterns to reuse in a talking-head explainer
1. **One shape, many roles** -> a script that moves problem -> product -> features -> sign-off. Pick one simple shape; let it be the timeline marker, the chart unit, the shrunken screen and finally the logo dot. Every screen enters by growing out of it (about 10 frames) and leaves by shrinking back into it (10 frames, darkening as it shrinks).
2. **Break the problem word, rebuild as the answer** -> "X is broken, so we built Y". Show X alone for about 2 s, tilt two middle letters, let all letters tumble for about 1 s, re-form as Y over about 1 s, hold 1 s still.
3. **Year counter on a hairline** -> "like going back to ...", "since 2015". Dots slide along a line while a small year label counts; 2 s for the whole count, big steps in the middle, small at the end.
4. **Screen + two-line caption** -> naming a feature. White panel on one side built from sliding rectangles (0.9 s), rows filling top to bottom; beside it a bold verb and a regular second line ("Manage / your invoices") at about 6% frame height. For a list, stack the caption pairs as each is spoken and shift the block up.
5. **Split to many, merge to one** -> "all your accounts in one place", "without switching". One square splits into 2x2 (0.5 s), holds 0.5 s, merges (0.5 s), then grows into the screen.
6. **Hub with orbiting logos** -> "works with / connects to ...". Product initial in the centre of thin rings, 3-4 partner logos fly in from the edges in about 13 frames and drift; fade the group and bring in the next group when the category word changes.
7. **Disorder to order** -> "stop juggling ..., start ...". Misaligned bars slide back and forth for about 2.5 s, snap into one clean block in about 0.25 s on the word "start", then thin outlines ripple outward.
