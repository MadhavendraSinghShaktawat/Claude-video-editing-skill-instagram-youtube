## Look and use of graphics

Written from `reference/analysis/ritesh-ai-agency/analysis.md` (every shot sheet read). By eye: screen recording 36 %, speaker full frame 28 %, speaker card beside a graphic 12 %, full-screen diagram 11 %.

- **Graphic system**: white page with a vignette (`#fefefe` centre, `#d0d0d0` corners) or a white-to-grey gradient (`#ffffff` to `#bdbdbd`); black and grey only, red used for "wrong"; boxes with thin outlines; heavy italic for emphasis; dark cards for prices; flat 2D.
- **Signature layout**: the speaker as a portrait card (26 % of frame width, 71 % of height, rounded, at x 0.685 to 0.948) with the graphic in the left 65 %; 18 uses, about 6 s each. The speaker shrinks into the card over 0.55 to 0.95 s. A white panel slides over the left half (0.9 s in, 0.7 s out) for quick lists.
- **Camera**: system C in `skills/camera.md`. 20 take joins left as visible jump cuts; 6 punches in 16 minutes; slow drift of 0.2 to 0.8 % per second on long shots; tighter at each cut through the closing argument.
- **Transitions**: hard cuts, except the card shrink, the panel slide, a blur cross-dissolve of 11 frames between proofs, and one 4-frame white flash.
- **Sections**: 10 numbered sections, each opened by the same title card: "N/10" at 10 % of frame height, a black bar wiping out from a cursor line, the name in white inside it; build 1.9 s.
- **Diagrams**: all parts shown as faint ghosts at the cut, each turning dark within 0.3 s of its noun while the previous one dims; in a wide flowchart the camera zooms about x1.15 and follows the active box; a bold pay-off line types on at the end.
- **Other material**: b-roll of the creator doing the thing described, 1.5 to 3.5 s per clip, hard cut on the first word of the clause, silent (8 inserts). Screen recordings at 88 % size on the gradient page with a round speaker inset; no zooms or highlights; waiting removed by a hard cut.
- **Text**: captions over footage only in the hook (two blocks beside the face, typed 0.10 to 0.13 s per word); after that all text sits on pages and panels.
- **Sound**: the "hook only" setting in `skills/sound.md`. Music at 5 to 15 s and 25 to 35 s; four placed effects in the first 36 s; bare voice for the remaining 15.6 minutes.
