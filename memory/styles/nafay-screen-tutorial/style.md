## Look and use of graphics

Written from `reference/analysis/nafay-faceless-shorts/analysis.md` (every shot sheet and an even overview read). The creator speaks Hindi; word counts above come from the English dub.

- **Two parts**: a designed intro of 22.5 s (3.3 % of runtime), then 650 s of screen recording shown raw and full frame. The speaker is not seen after 22.5 s.
- **Intro**: two speaker layouts, full frame (face 41 % of frame height) for sentences aimed at the viewer and a portrait picture-in-picture on the right (21 % x 53 % of the frame, rounded, light-blue border) for sentences that name something drawable; the clip is scaled between them over 0.37 to 0.43 s, never cut. Five layout changes in 22 s. Graphics on an off-white canvas `#f6f4f2`, accent blue `#4a96ee`, green for yes, red for no; a serif title pill whose last word is blue; one element per spoken phrase; repeated items staggered 0.13 s; a finished panel held 0.5 to 0.7 s then cleared in 0.20 s.
- **Tutorial body**: no zooms, boxes, arrows, blur, captions, padding, music or effects. Playback stays at 1.0x. About half of the recorded time is removed by cuts made while the screen is static. Each wait is removed with one hard cut and bridged in speech. A new step is marked only by the hard cut to the other tool. Pointing is done with the mouse in the recording.
- **Edits after the intro**: about 1.1 a minute; 50 to 120 s with no visible edit is normal. The zooms measured at 189 to 223 s are the image viewer inside the recording, not the editor.
- **One overlay** in the body: a black box correcting a spoken slip, 0.75 s.
- **Sound**: no music. Possible quiet effects on the intro graphics (six candidates, not confirmed because they overlap speech).
