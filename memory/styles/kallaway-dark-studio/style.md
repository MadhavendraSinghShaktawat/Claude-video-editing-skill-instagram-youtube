## Look and use of graphics

Written from `reference/analysis/kallaway-dopamine-ladders/analysis.md` (every shot sheet read, camera scale checked by matching the background).

- **Graphic system**: near-black canvas (`#010101` centre to `#252227` edges) with a violet glow in the bottom corners (`#5d0bba`); one accent violet `#b577f3` on text; white and light grey geometric sans; rounded cards with a violet-to-dark gradient and a thin light outline; flat 2D with glow.
- **Camera**: system A in `skills/camera.md`. Locked camera; the speaker appears at four sizes only (0.953, 1.000, 1.047, 1.095). Punch cut of one level at a take join that starts a sentence (27 times). Eased zoom of two levels over 0.46 s about every 37 s of speaker time; zoom out before overlays appear beside the head, back in when they leave. The "slow pull out" share in the table above is a measuring error; there is one real slow move in the video.
- **Into and out of a graphic**: hard cut to an empty canvas at the start of a sentence, parts build over 0.3 to 1.2 s, hard cut back. No wipes. Speaker and graphic alternate in segments of about 10 s; 61 % speaker, 39 % graphic.
- **Signature diagram**: a ladder with one rung per level, visited 12 times (13 % of runtime). Every section change: one-line recap on the speaker, hard cut on "All right", camera climbs one rung in 1.4 s, the name appears within 1 s of being said, hold 3 to 11 s, cut back to the widest speaker framing. Recaps after two thirds and at the end.
- **Text**: no running captions. Terms in heavy white caps, 7.6 to 16 % of frame height, on the word. Lower-third takeaways at x 0.10, y 0.77 to 0.88, about one every 90 s, held about 8 s. Side labels left and right of the head. Questions typed on the blurred, dimmed speaker (3 times).
- **Sound**: the "dry" setting in `skills/sound.md`. No music. A short low sound (about 0.2 s, 50 to 90 Hz, 3 to 6 dB under the voice) on 22 of 26 eased zooms and on fast moves inside graphics; nothing on punch cuts, hard cuts or text. Master about -14 LUFS.
